## The finding of 24 Nov (dh610, credited to this runner; fixed by the coordinator at feat/inner-mp 20ad2589)
- **Symptom:** the first extended-precision point of attempt 3, (A 3.3192059515625, wf 0.22529321), had been valued twice in attempt 2 (cache rows 3641 and 3646) and was valued again. Its float64 row is 3639, keyed A 3.319205951562; the mp rows say A 3.319205951563.
- **Cause:** `Multiplier.key(p)` is `(round(p[0], 12), round(p[1], 12))`. The walk's points are numpy float64 (as the `repr` bug of attempt 1 showed), whose `round` gives 3.319205951562 for the decimal tie 3.3192059515625 (checked: numpy float64 round 3.319205951562, Python float round 3.319205951563; the same for 3.5526043890625). `mp_batch` took the key from the returned row's `A`, a Python float, so it stored the value under ...563 while the walk and `mp_wanted` look under ...562: the walk never saw the value, and `mp_wanted` (float64 row, `fu` not mp) asked for the point again at every request, 1000 to 2000 s each.
- **Extent in the cache** (analysis of the 3847 lines): 2 distinct tie points: (3.3192059515625, 0.22529321), float64 row 3639, mp rows 3641, 3646, 3846, 3847 under ...563; (3.5526043890625, 0.41279321), float64 row 3598, mp row 3797 under ...563. Of the 138 mp rows before attempt 3's two, 134 share their key with a float64 row; the other 4 are these tie rows. So the 107 mp evaluations of attempt 2 include 1 repeat, and the walk saw no value at the two tie points. No wrong value was ever stored; the effect is wasted evaluations and a possible unresolved leaf where the walk needed a tie point.
