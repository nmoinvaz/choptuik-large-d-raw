# I39-mac: independent review of the lemma 6b1(5) and 6a(5) certificates, regenerated disc inputs and logs

Machine: mac (Apple M5 Mac, 10 cores, macOS 27), /Users/nathan/venv-qd (Python 3.13.15, python-flint 0.9.0 with
FLINT 3.6.0, mpmath 1.3.0 with the pure-Python backend, numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.18.1). Issue:
https://github.com/nmoinvaz/choptuik-large-d/issues/39. Code: solver/proof/review_39/ on branch wip/i39.
At most 2 processes, nice 10.

- disc_inputs/row_JJ.json: the exact arguments (centre, Lohner box, w ball, r, n) that lemma6b1_cover_M.row_run passes
  to lemma6b1_disc_M.certify for each disc of row JJ, regenerated by review_39/dump_inputs.py from the stored C^1 ray of
  I38-macm3/lemma6b1_runs_M5_cert/ray.json (the stored rows keep only the verdicts), with the original certify's
  verdict on them. dump_inputs_*.log: its output.
- check_6b1/row_JJ.json, check_6b1_*.log: the review's own disc lemma (mpmath intervals) on those inputs.
- check_6a_*.{log,json}, flat_6a_M5.json (export_6a_flat.py), orig_6a_*.log, B_M5_sub1e-18_<mutation>.json: lemma 6a(5)'s
  final step re-evaluated from I103-macm3's (and I104-win2's) B, its mutations, and the original on the mutated B.
- b_float*.log: the double-precision control of B (w at x^ and at p^). lohner_float*.log: the double-precision control
  of the arc boxes (the _v1_with_ray logs are the first version, which fired because its own double-precision error
  along the ray exceeded the ray box). ray_hp*.log: the 192-bit Taylor control of the ray boxes (ray_hp_v1_intervals.log
  is the first version, which fired because it carried interval widths grown by wrapping).
- orig_cover_*.log: the original covering check, clean and mutated (controls.py orig_cover).
- mut_sign_row*.log, rscale_row*.log and orig_*: the disc mutations in the review and in the original.
- summary_6b1.md: the per-row table. cover_6b1.{log,json}, cover_6b1_mut_*.log: the review's C1-C8, clean and mutated.
- scripts/: the shell scripts that ran everything (worker.sh ran two workers over the rows; the others the controls and
  mutations); paths to the session's scratch directory are where ray.json was linked and helper logs went.

the review's C1-C8 pass (366 tiles, 1188 pieces, 957 links, as the original); drop 9,5, row 9 r x 1.01 and G0 x 1.01
fail in both codes; the sign mutation certifies no disc in either code. The regenerated boxes equal the stored radii bit
for bit on 274 of 366 discs; on rows 0-2 they differ by up to 6% from disc 3-4 on, with the same step counts. 6a(5):
rho_6a <= 0.0790076 from I103's B (original 0.231, loose acb_mat.inv); the run used w centred at x^ = ZEROS[5], not p^
(7.9e-10 away), so B is not proven at the certificate's parameters; b_float puts the p^ flow at 0.178 of B's radius.
