# I200-mac: search and read logs of the literature search on the superconducting threshold, mean-field exponents, RG limit cycles and viscosity, read beside critical collapse at large D

Machine: Apple M5 Mac, a subagent of the coordinator.
Issue: https://github.com/nmoinvaz/choptuik-large-d/issues/200

- `search_log.md`: every search, with its engine, query, date and what it surfaced.
- `read_log.md`: every source opened, with its URL, the part read, and short quotes as the fetch tool returned them.
  Citations and short quotes only, no full texts.

The verdicts per question are in notes/LIT_REVIEW.md of the main repository (addendum of 2026-11-09, issue #200).
