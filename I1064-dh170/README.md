Script `scalar_scan1064.py` (same setup as before, one LU). Reading rules, fixed now:
1. Roots of the weak scalar function: a sign change of g_1 along v_1 (or g_2 along v_2) between neighbouring decades on one side, refined by bisection in log t to a relative 1e-9; a sign change between t = 0 (g = a_i) and the smallest |t| is reported as such. 2. The signed even part q_i(t) = (g_i(t) + g_i(-t) - 2 a_i) / t^2 (the second derivative of the projection, where it is resolved) and the odd part (g_i(t) - g_i(-t)) / (2 t) (sigma_i at small t, the cubic term's size at large t) are printed per decade. q_i is called resolved only in a window where two neighbouring decades agree to 10% and |q_i| t^2 is at least 1e6 times the smallest |even part| seen in the scan (the noise reference); else it is reported as 'not resolved' with its bound (the noise level over t^2).
