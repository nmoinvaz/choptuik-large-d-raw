## Reading, fixed before (t5's), and what the leg can and cannot show
- **Converged:** the k_c 87 weakest mode's peak lies within 0.06 of 21.03125 (D 20.97 to 21.09). **Not converged:** it peaks 0.1 or more earlier than 21.03125 (D 20.93 or below). In between (0.06 to 0.1 earlier) is neither; I report it as such.
- **A limit of this leg, stated before:** the seeds' records (regrids by dh170 and dh610) carry no singular values, so the tracked mode's sigma exists only from the leg's first new point, D 21.03125 (and its ratio to the previous sigma and its tangent cos from the second, D 21.0625). A peak at or after D 21.03125 shows as a rise then a fall inside the leg; a peak before the first point shows as a fall from the first row on, and then the leg says "at or before 21.03125", not how much earlier. If that happens I report it so and name what would separate the two cases (sigma at the seeds' D, which the leap's step does not compute).
