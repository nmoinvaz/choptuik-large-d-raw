## The two-mode fit with D-linear slopes: law and pass criteria, written before the code (2026-11-20 18:52 UTC; coordinator's hc 18:5x UTC, after the D 260.8483 crossing state)
Why: the constant-slope law of `twomode949.py` (kept, unchanged) misplaces the crossing (D_c 260.795 predicted, about 260.85 measured) and misses the share trajectory when the crossing state is in the window (fitted 0.27 against 0.498, `crossing_D260.8483.txt`). New code only: `twomode_lin1057.py`.
- **Law** (x = D − D_ref, D_ref the last state of the window): two diabatic branches, the Δ branch and the second branch, each with a log slope linear in D: ln a_i = p_i − s_i x − c_i x²/2 (i = Δ, 2). Coupling g = γ sqrt(a_Δ a_2), 0 ≤ γ ≤ 0.9. The observed pair is the eigenvalues of [[a_Δ, g], [g, a_2]]: σ_lowest the minus root, σ_upper the plus root. The Δ branch is the one that is lower at the first state of the window (the lowest vector carries 0.99 of the Δ mode there). Seven parameters (p_Δ, s_Δ, c_Δ, p_2, s_2, c_2, γ). Least squares on ln σ_lowest and ln σ_upper at every state of the window (the two lowest singular values of the state's records, vectors 1 and 2, with no classification), a grid of starts. The measured shares are not fitted: they are the check.
- **Windows**: A, the six states D 259.7352 to 260.8483 (12 values for 7 parameters, 5 degrees of freedom), is the one the criteria are applied to. Sensitivity, reported and not judged: B, five states D 260.0073 to 260.8483, and C, seven states D 259.4568 to 260.8483.
- **Pass criteria for window A (the coordinator's):** (1) the fitted Δ share of the lowest vector is within 0.1 of the measured Δ share of vector 1 at every state of the window, and the complement (the upper vector's) within 0.1 of the measured share of vector 2, the crossing state's 0.498 and 0.494 included; (2) the model's gap at its own crossing, (1 + γ)/(1 − γ), is within 20% of the measured σ_upper/σ_lowest = 1.160 at D 260.8483, that is in [0.928, 1.392]; the model's ratio at D 260.8483 is reported with it. Also reported with the fit, pass or not: the residuals of ln σ_lowest and ln σ_upper at every state, the fitted and measured shares of both vectors, all seven parameters and D_c.
